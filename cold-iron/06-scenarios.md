# 6. Scenarios

*Part of the [rules draft](README.md). Tags: **[Proposed]** = awaiting a yes or no. **[Open Q#]** = see [open-questions.md](open-questions.md).*

## 6.1 The Table

The board is **3' x 3' (36" x 36")**.

**[Proposed, Voidwatch §0.1]** Use dense terrain with plenty of verticality. Mix open, exposed spaces with tight, cramped ones. Variety matters more than looks.

## 6.2 Warbands

- **5-15 models.** 1 Leader, 1 Champion and 3 Henchmen are required. The rest is optional.
- A 15-model warband is rare and means fragile models (FERAL beasts) that are only good if they deliver the first blow.
- How warbands are balanced against each other (points, rating): **[Open Q11]**.

## 6.3 Game Length

- The game lasts **4 rounds**.
- At the end of round 4, roll a d6. On a flat **4+**, play a 5th round. The game never goes past 5.
- Players do not know how long the game will last.
- **[Proposed]** Score objectives at the end of every round, so an early finish is fair to both sides.

## 6.4 Deployment

- Each player deploys their **initial group** within **12" of opposite corners**.
- Most scenarios split a warband into **two or three groups**. Only the initial group uses the 12" zone.
- Each **additional group** deploys at a **randomly determined insert point**. This speeds up setup and makes games less predictable.

**[Proposed]** procedure. **[Open Q20]**

1. Before deploying, each player secretly assigns every model to a group.
2. For each additional group, roll two d6. One picks the column and the other the row of a 3 x 3 grid of 12" squares (1-2, 3-4, 5-6). Reroll if the result is either player's corner square.
3. The opponent places the insert marker in that square, at least 6" from any enemy model.
4. The group deploys with every model within 3" of the marker.

**[Proposed, Voidwatch §0.2]** Roll a d6 each. The high roll picks a table corner or who deploys first.

## 6.5 Neutral Threats (PvPvE)

Every scenario places **neutral threats**: models that belong to neither player. **[Proposed, Open Q21]**

- Neutrals act as a third side in a **Neutral Phase at the end of each round**.
- They follow a fixed behavior rule, based on Voidwatch §13.3: take whichever action is most detrimental, attack the nearest model of either warband, move by the safest and shortest route, use cover when available. They are competent, not omniscient.
- Ambiguous choices are made by the player who is behind on score. On a tie, the player who lost initiative this round decides.
- Neutrals use the same profile format as everyone else.

## 6.6 Scenario template [Proposed]

Each scenario lists: objective, table setup, groups and insert points, neutral threats, special rules, scoring and campaign rewards.
