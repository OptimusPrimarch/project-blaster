# 5. Terrain and Movement

*Part of the [rules draft](README.md). Tags: **[Proposed]** = awaiting a yes or no. **[Open Q#]** = see [open-questions.md](open-questions.md).*

## 5.1 Line of Sight [Proposed, Voidwatch §4.1]

A model has line of sight (LoS) to a target if a straight line can be traced from any part of it to any part of the target without passing through terrain. When in doubt, crouch to table level.

Cover is defined in [Combat](02-combat.md).

## 5.2 Difficult Terrain [Proposed, Voidwatch §4.3]

Difficult terrain (rubble, water, undergrowth) costs double movement.

## 5.3 Climbing [Proposed, Voidwatch §4.4, Open Q15]

Climbing can be part of any MOVE.

- Every 1" climbed, up or down, costs 2" of movement.
- A model cannot climb a surface unless it can end its move standing on top of it.
- When it finishes climbing, place it on top in base contact with the edge it climbed (or the nearest legal spot). Any remaining movement can be used as normal.

## 5.4 Ladders

A ladder connects two levels.

1. A model must **end its movement within 1"** of the ladder (at either end).
2. It may then, optionally, **spend all of its remaining movement** (if any) to traverse the ladder immediately. It is placed within 1" of the other end.

So a model that used its full Speed to reach a ladder can still traverse it. Traversing the ladder ends that MOVE.

- Ladders ignore the 2-for-1 climbing cost.
- **[Proposed]** Ladders work in both directions.
- Restrictions (large bases, enemies near the top, one model at a time): **[Open Q14]**.

*Example.* A Cutthroat (SPD 5") moves 5" and ends within 1" of a ladder. It has no movement left but may still traverse: it is placed within 1" of the top.

## 5.5 Vantage

For ranged attacks, the attacker gains **BOON 1 for each full 3" it is above the target**.

- **[Proposed]** Measure the height difference between the surfaces the two bases stand on.
- With the cap in 1.1 **[Open Q2]**, two levels (6") is the most that matters.
- **[Proposed]** Build terrain in 3" levels where practical, so vantage reads straight off the table.

## 5.6 Falling [Proposed, Open Q15]

A model that falls (for example, pushed off a ledge) takes 1 damage per full 3" fallen, with no save.
