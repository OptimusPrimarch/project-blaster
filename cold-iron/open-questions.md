# Open Questions

*Part of the [rules draft](README.md). Each item has a recommended default. Items marked ★ are most likely to block a first playtest.*

## Core and actions

**Q1. Hands.** The draft assumes 3 hands, with 1-handed items costing 1 and 2-handed items costing 2. What does a torch do (light, fire, something else)?
*Recommend:* keep 3 hands. Decide the torch effect with the scenario rules.

**Q2. BOON/BANE cap.** Should the net be capped at 2?
*Recommend:* yes. Without it, stacked sources push saves toward invulnerable.

**Q3. ★ Strike timing.** Does each strike resolve (save, then damage) as soon as it is allocated, or do all dice get allocated first?
*Recommend:* immediately. It makes the first blow matter (which FERAL beasts rely on) and means nothing to track.

**Q4. Crits and saves.** Does a crit hit get an armor save?
*Recommend:* yes. A crit is dangerous because of its damage, not because it cannot be saved.

**Q5. ★ Charge.** CHARGE (full: 2x Speed, then FIGHT) beats MOVE + FIGHT, because it fits double the movement and a fight into one activation. Options: (a) accept it, (b) require a straight path with no climbing or ladders, (c) give the charger a bonus.
*Recommend:* (b), then see how playtests go.

**Q6. ★ Leaving engagement.** What does it cost to leave melee?
*Recommend:* a full-action RETREAT (move up to Speed, ending not engaged). It keeps melee sticky without reactions or free strikes.

**Q7. Shooting while engaged.**
*Recommend:* allowed at BANE 1.

**Q8. ★ Ranged weapons.** The draft uses melee's dice tiers (2/3/4), no range bands and no range limit beyond line of sight. Ranged attacks cannot be parried, so they probably need fewer dice or lower damage than melee. Do thrown weapons need a max range?
*Recommend:* keep no range bands. Tune bows and crossbows by damage.

**Q9. Henchmen groups.** Should identical Henchmen within 3" activate together (Voidwatch §13.2)?
*Recommend:* yes. It keeps big games fast.

**Q10. ★ Initiative and uneven counts.** Who chooses to go first? 5 models vs 15 means one side runs out of activations early (Voidwatch gives free Overwatch tokens).
*Recommend:* the player with fewer models chooses each round. Decide on compensation after playtests.

**Q11. ★ Warband balance.** There is no cost system yet. 15 fragile beasts vs 5 elites needs a budget.
*Recommend:* points per model with a total budget, and a "warband rating" for campaigns.

**Q12. Mettle tests.** When are they called for?
*Recommend:* when the Leader is slain (each Henchman tests), for scenario interactions, and against fear effects from neutrals.

**Q13. Base size.** 25-60mm bases: what do they change mechanically?
*Recommend:* footprint only, plus a ladder restriction for bases over 40mm.

**Q23. Wounds for Champions, Leaders and monsters.** You set Henchmen at about 3 and beasts at about 2. The draft's examples use Champion 5 and Leader 6. What range do you want?
*Recommend:* Champions 5, Leaders 6, large monsters 8+. Check against damage: weapons deal 1-3 and crits go up to 7 (D6+1).

## Terrain

**Q14. Ladders.** Restrictions for large bases, enemies near the top, and how many models can use one?
*Recommend:* no limit beyond "the base must fit within 1" of the other end", plus no ladders for bases over 40mm.

**Q15. Climbing and falling.** The draft reuses Voidwatch's 2-for-1 climbing and adds 1 damage per full 3" fallen. What about voluntary drops?
*Recommend:* a deliberate drop of up to 3" costs no extra movement. Anything higher is a fall.

**Q16. Blasts.** Confirm the method: SHOOT test for a direct hit, otherwise d6" scatter in a d6 direction, fixed damage, friendly fire.
*Alternative:* Voidwatch §9.1, where successes reduce a fixed scatter distance.

## Magic

**Q17. Spells.** How do casters get spells (a fixed list, a choice, a grimoire)? Do armor saves apply to spell damage? Is Full the right default cost? Should "TN" be renamed (Voidwatch uses Strain) to avoid colliding with X+ target numbers?
*Recommend:* fixed spell list per caster, saves allowed, Full by default, rename to avoid the clash.

**Q18. BACKBLAST table.** Placeholder entries only. Needs real design and flavor.

## Equipment

**Q19. Held items and full plate.**
- You wrote that a great shield imposes "boon 1 on your own melee attacks". The draft assumes **BANE 1**, since it is a drawback. Please confirm.
- Great shield: how big is the Save bonus (+2?), and how many hands?
- Buckler: BOON 1 on saves like chainmail? (Shields and bucklers are held, so each takes a hand.)
- Full plate: the draft adds -1" Speed. Any other drawback? (Cost is Q11.)
- Allow only one shield-type item per model?
*Recommend:* BANE 1; great shield +2 and 1 hand; buckler BOON 1; -1" Speed on full plate; one shield-type item per model.

**Q22. IRON scope.** The draft makes chainmail, breastplate and full plate IRON: each costs a caster 1 Willpower die, and a caster reduced to 0 dice cannot cast. Is a helm IRON? Are shields? Per piece, or once if any IRON is worn?
*Recommend:* per piece; the helm is IRON (it makes the helm a real choice for casters); shields are not unless iron-faced.

## Scenarios

**Q20. ★ Additional groups.** Deploy at setup or arrive later? How are insert points rolled (grid, scatter from an anchor, edge roll)? How close to enemies? Are group assignments secret?
*Recommend:* deploy at setup, grid roll with the opponent placing the marker, 6" from enemies, assignments secret.

**Q21. Neutrals.** Timing (end of round vs interleaved), who resolves ambiguous choices, whether they score or give XP.
*Recommend:* end-of-round phase, trailing player decides ambiguities, kills give XP only in campaigns.
