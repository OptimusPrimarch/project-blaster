# Cold Iron: Rules Draft

**Cold Iron** (working title) is a d6 skirmish game for 5-15 models a side, set in a wyrd, low-magic, gritty (not grimdark) dark-fantasy world. The setting is deliberately vague and agnostic. Magic is rare and risky. Every scenario puts neutral threats on the table (PvPvE).

This is a **work in progress**. Expect gaps and changes.

## How to read this draft

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *(no tag)* | Decided by the designer. |
| **[Proposed]** | A suggestion, or a rule reused from Voidwatch, waiting for a yes or no. |
| **[Open Q#]** | Unresolved. The question and a recommended default are in [open-questions.md](open-questions.md). |

Keywords are written in CAPS (BOON, BANE, RENDING, LAYERED), matching the Voidwatch rules.

## At a glance

- **Dice:** d6, X+ to succeed. A natural 1 always fails and a natural 6 always succeeds, overriding every modifier.
- **BOON / BANE:** extra dice that help or hurt a roll.
- **Warband:** 5-15 models. 1 Leader, 1 Champion and 3 Henchmen are required. The rest is optional.
- **Board:** 3' x 3' (36" x 36"). Initial deployment within 12" of opposite corners.
- **Length:** 4 rounds, with a chance of a 5th.
- **Turns:** alternating activations. Each model gets two half-actions, or one full action.
- **Melee:** both sides roll, then alternate allocating hits to STRIKE or PARRY (Kill Team-style). Armor saves shrug damage.
- **Magic:** the only place successes are counted. High risk (BACKBLAST), high reward.
- **PvPvE:** neutral enemies in every scenario.
- **Campaign:** XP, promotions and skills.

## Relationship to Voidwatch

This game is separate from Voidwatch (the 4d10, success-counting game in the repo root). Where a Voidwatch rule is reused as a starting point, it is tagged **[Proposed]** and cites its section, for example "Voidwatch §4.4".
