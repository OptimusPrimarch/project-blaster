# 7. Campaign

*Part of the [rules draft](README.md). Stub: not yet designed.*

## Planned scope

- XP gain
- Promotions
- Skill acquisition

## Starting ideas [Proposed, unreviewed]

- XP comes from objectives and notable kills (Champions, neutral threats), not from every fight, to limit snowballing.
- A model that is slain rolls on an injury table after the game: fine, scarred or dead.
- A model that reaches an XP threshold is promoted and picks a skill from a short list tied to its role.
- A small catch-up bonus for the warband with the lowest rating.
