# Design Preferences

My own stated preferences for the new game. Claude records these but never infers or invents them. Each entry is dated. If I change my mind, add a new dated line and mark the old one `superseded`; don't delete it.

## Confirmed

### Format & scale
- 2026-11-06 | Play modes: **1v1** and **co-op** (co-op needs a way to drive the enemy side, e.g. scripted or card/dice-driven).
- 2026-11-06 | Target playtime: **60-90 min**, setup included.
- 2026-11-06 | Typical force: **7-12 models per player**.

### Feel
- 2026-11-06 | Lethality: **moderate** (a few hits to take a model out; some survive wounded).
- 2026-11-06 | Luck vs. skill: **balanced** (good tactics help, but upsets and swings happen regularly).
- 2026-11-06 | Rules complexity: **medium** (teach in about 30 minutes; a quick-reference sheet covers most lookups).

### Structure
- 2026-11-06 | Activation: **card/token/dice-driven** (a draw or roll decides who acts next). Open point: exactly how this interacts with the 1v1 and co-op modes. Revisit after the pilot games are ingested.
- 2026-11-06 | Progression: **later module**. Design a strong one-off game first, add campaign rules afterward.

### Project scope
- 2026-11-06 | Ingest **core rules only** for each game. Faction/army books, army lists, and campaign supplements are out of scope for now.
- 2026-11-06 | Kill Team means the **2024 edition**. Trench Crusade is a living ruleset (first official edition, updated over time): record the version/date of the file ingested, and re-ingest when it changes.
